What should a household
promise to trade?

A forecast is a prediction. A commitment is a promise. A meter reading determines how much energy can actually be settled.

Explore six synthetic households: three homes offer spare solar energy and three want to buy it. Change their preferences or forecasts, run the agents, and see what gets agreed and paid.

The research questionHow can customer agents respect individual preferences, make decisions under uncertainty, and coordinate with utility constraints? This lab demonstrates the trading workflow with rules. It does not yet compare smarter policies.
01

Households decide

Sellers keep a reserve and name a minimum price. Buyers set demand, a price ceiling and a spending limit.

02

The market checks

Compatible offers match. An aggregate utility trading cap limits commitments, and buyers reserve payment.

03

Meters determine payment

After commitment, simulated meter readings limit settlement. Unused payment is released; missing readings keep trades pending.

One shared synthetic trading interval. Energy is measured in kWh; 1 kWh is one unit. Prices are INR per kWh. The utility cap is a trading limit, not an electrical network model.

What this demonstrates

Deterministic agents running through Nandatown’s messaging and ledger, bounded by structured preferences. No LLM calls, real customer data, live Tata connection, device control, or power-flow calculations.

What comes next

Compare static, forecast-based, uncertainty-aware, centralized and hindsight policies. A later language interface could translate preferences into validated policies.

Research context

Based on the project’s proposed design package and October 2026 meeting material. The broader program spans consumer agents, utility coordination and population studies. P2P trading is the immediate demonstrator, not a confirmed exclusive pilot.
